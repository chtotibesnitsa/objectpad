# ObjectPad asset register

All artwork in this pack is editorial illustration, not user capture evidence or a live token. No network-owner logo, feather or mark is used. The network relationship appears only as plain text with a non-affiliation disclosure.

## Delivered locally

- Three generated GLB objects: terracotta amphora, river stone, walnut stool. The amphora is integrated in the lazy-loaded interactive process viewer; the other two are delivered for future editorial use.
- Eight object-category illustrations, cropped from a generated editorial atlas.
- Twelve generated navigation glyph tiles.
- Six capture-to-record process illustrations.
- Six mineral-specimen avatars.
- Empty, loading, failure and success state artwork.
- Four text-free social background templates.
- Cloud/vase hero at 1280 and 640 pixels; subtle topographic background.
- Eight original UI sound effects, compressed locally, off by default.
- A cube-derived interim favicon/app/profile/DEX image from the generated navigation atlas.
- A hand-authored cube/wordmark rendered in the application's `Brand` component.

## Important incomplete brand exports

The dedicated icon and Open Graph generators rejected the unregistered application path. Therefore there is no successfully generated primary brand icon, approved OG card, or final X banner. The interim icon is not represented as an approved final logo. Static SVG wordmark exports could not be written with the available asset tooling. Complete and approve those exports in the independent workspace before publishing.

No share-image metadata points to an invented public domain. The final domain and approved card must be configured together.

## Visual system

- Mineral white canvas `#f3f2ec`; graphite ink `#252b27`.
- Moss accent `#586b48`; terracotta `#af6749`; sky grey `#e5edf0`.
- Large, tight editorial headings; small widely tracked monospaced technical labels.
- Fine rules, restrained rounded containers, tangible materials and no financial-performance imagery.
- Motion respects the system reduced-motion setting. WebGL starts only after an explicit viewer interaction. No sound until opt-in.
